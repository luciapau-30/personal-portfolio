hey, i build!

here's some of my favorite projects i've worked on / am working on, and here's why i enjoy them!

smartpantry: this was a team project where i learned a lot, from working on algorithms (which i love!) to navigating challenges within a team. i worked on the recommendation algorithms and backend logic, and also had the opportunity to present our project alongside another team member!

carpoolz: if there's something my friends and family know about me, it's that joining the USC Ski and Snowboard team was one of the best decisions i've made since coming to USC. for last year's e-board elections, i pitched a new carpool coordination system. each weekend, someone has to coordinate rides for around 50 people, matching each person with a car up the mountain and another back down. this system uses a deterministic algorithm to match riders with available cars based on their preferred departure times and days, while accounting for vehicle capacity.

piece of mind: this project is personal to me, which is why i'm sharing it even though it's still in progress. i was diagnosed with celiac disease almost 10 years ago, and figuring out what to eat or buy when i'm away from home has always been a challenge. i'm building this project to help people with allergies, dietary restrictions, or dietary preferences figure out what to order or buy with a little less stress.
