about me

I grew up in Panama and now live in Los Angeles. I’m interested in understanding how software and systems work, from the algorithms behind them to the ways people use them. I especially enjoy building products that can make everyday life a little easier.

Before computer science, gymnastics shaped the way I approach every challenge I face. As a five-time member of Panama’s national team, I learned to stay determined through everything, respond to feedback, and keep showing up no matter what, especially when progress doesn’t feel linear.

Today, I bring those same values and mindset into building software and teaching. At USC, I am a Learning Assistant for the Technology and Applied Computing program (TAC), helping students make their first steps into computer science. Additionally, I work with USC’s Student Athlete Academic Services, tutoring student-athletes in STEM subjects.

Last summer, I worked at KPMG as a technology consulting intern. I helped develop a roadmap for a core banking transformation at a leading Latin American bank, automated a month-long shift-allocation process down to a few hours, and built a centralized data pipeline to consolidate project information across consulting teams. Outside of work, I began working on my CompTIA Security+ certification, and spent the rest of my time with family and friends, finding new beaches to surf around home, and training for a half marathon.
